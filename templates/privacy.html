Privacy Policy

How personal information is handled on this website, in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA). Last updated {{ legal_updated }}.

1. Who is responsible

The responsible party for this website is {{ s.hero.name }}, an individual based in {{ s.contact.location or 'South Africa' }}. For privacy questions or requests, write to {{ s.contact.email }}. As an individual I am also my own information officer.

2. What is collected, why, and for how long

Contact form and messages

If you use the contact form I receive your name, email address and message. I use them only to read and reply to your enquiry. The message is processed by the form service Formspree (see section 4) and delivered to my email. I keep it only as long as needed to deal with your enquiry and for up to 12 months afterwards, then delete it. The same applies if you email me directly or message me on WhatsApp.

Visit statistics (first-party, no cookies)

To see which pages are useful, this site's own server records for each page view: the page, the date and time, the referring website (domain only), the device type (mobile, tablet or desktop) and an anonymous visitor code. The visitor code is a one-way hash of your IP address and browser details that changes every day. It cannot be turned back into your IP address, and it lets me count unique visitors per day without storing your IP address or following you across days. Known search-engine and other automated visitors are not counted. These statistics are kept for up to 13 months and are never sold or shared with advertisers.

Server logs

Like every web server, the server that hosts this site records technical details of each request (your IP address, time, the page requested and your browser type) in access logs. These are used only for security and fault-finding and are rotated out after about two weeks.

3. Cookies and similar technologies

This website does not set any cookies for visitors, and does not use advertising, social-media tracking or third-party analytics. A sign-in cookie exists only for the site owner's administration area. The SecurePulse demo dashboard saves a name that you type into it in your own browser's local storage so that it can greet you; this stays on your device and is never sent to me. You can clear it at any time by signing out of the demo or clearing your browser data.

All fonts, icons and scripts used by this site are served from the same server, so your browser does not contact Google, Cloudflare or other third parties just to display the pages.

5. Security

The site is served over HTTPS, administration is protected by a hashed password and a restricted session, and only the minimum personal information needed is collected. No system is perfectly secure, but I will notify you and the Information Regulator if a breach affecting your information occurs, as POPIA requires.

6. Your rights

You may ask me to confirm what personal information I hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, or to stop processing it, and you may withdraw consent you have given. Email {{ s.contact.email }} and I will respond within 30 days.

7. Children

This website is not directed at children under 18 and I do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian before sending a message.

8. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how your information has been handled you may complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa): inforegulator.org.za/complaints. General enquiries: enquiries@inforegulator.org.za, 010 023 5200 or toll-free 0800 017 160.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date at the top.
